# Vendor implementations of an enterprise context layer for data and AI agents

Research date: 2026-10-26. Dates in parentheses are the publication or event dates of the cited source. When a source gave no reliable date, it is marked "undated". Statements from vendors, and from vendor-run blogs (Atlan, AtScale, Strategy, Snowflake, Databricks, Google, Salesforce), are labelled as vendor claims. Many 2026 event recaps come from third parties whose status labels disagree. Those conflicts are recorded below and not silently resolved.

## What concrete mechanism does each vendor use to give agents business meaning, and how do agents consume it?

### Takeaway
The vendors fall into four groups. Warehouse and lakehouse platforms (Snowflake, Databricks, Google Looker, Salesforce Tableau) put governed semantic objects (semantic views, metric views, LookML, Tableau Semantics) next to the data. Catalog vendors (Atlan, Collibra, Alation) expose glossaries, lineage and certified definitions over MCP. Work-graph vendors (Glean, Microsoft Work IQ) index documents and people with permissions attached. Ontology and graph vendors (Palantir, Neo4j, Microsoft Fabric IQ) model entities and relationships. In 2026 MCP became the default way agents reach every one of these, so the differences now lie in the depth of the semantics and the permission model, not in whether an agent can connect.

### Inferences
- The mechanisms fall into three families that are converging. The first is metric-centric YAML semantic models (Snowflake semantic views, Databricks Metrics, dbt MetricFlow, LookML, Tableau Semantics). The second is entity-centric ontologies (Palantir, Fabric IQ, Genie Ontology, Neo4j). The third is document and people graphs (Glean, Work IQ). Databricks' Genie Ontology and Microsoft's Fabric IQ show warehouse vendors adding an ontology on top of their metric layers.
- Every vendor exposes its context over MCP, so MCP has become the baseline for agent access rather than a point of difference.
- Fewer vendors offer named financial services references than the scope requested. The ones found were PayPal (Google Looker MCP), Customers Bank (Palantir) and BlackRock (an OSI member, not a referenced customer).

### Gaps
- The retrieved sources did not cover several items from my brief. These are Snowflake Cortex Search and Cortex Analyst pricing, Databricks Lakebase, the data.world acquisition by ServiceNow, Microsoft Graph and the Copilot semantic index specifics, Purview's agent role, Amazon Kendra, Gemini Enterprise pricing and the SageMaker Unified Studio catalog. Primary sources should be fetched for each before relying on them.
- Public pricing was found only for Glean, and only its model, not a price list. Snowflake and Databricks context features are consumption-billed, but no retrieved source gave rates.
- No retrieved source named a financial services customer for Snowflake, Databricks, dbt, Atlan, Collibra, Alation, Glean, Microsoft, AWS or Salesforce context features. JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs and similar firms were not confirmed for any of these vendors.
- No 2026 funding round for a "context engineering platform" startup was found.

## What interoperability standards are emerging (OSI, MCP, OpenLineage)?

### Takeaway
OSI, now Apache Ossie (incubating), is the main attempt to make semantic definitions portable. It is a vendor-neutral YAML and JSON specification that is licensed under Apache 2.0. It defines datasets, metrics, dimensions, relationships and AI context. MCP is the de facto access protocol, and A2A is used for agent-to-agent hand-off. Lineage interoperability came up only indirectly: Databricks external lineage is GA and Collibra offers Lineage Read APIs. No retrieved source confirmed vendor OpenLineage commitments for 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **OSI launch.** Snowflake launched OSI in September 2025 with Salesforce, dbt Labs, BlackRock, RelationalAI and others as partners — [Snowflake blog (Sep 2025)](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/open-semantic-interchange-ai-standard/).
- **First specification.** The first version of the specification went live on 27 January 2026 under Apache 2.0 on GitHub. It defines a "vendor-neutral, extensible model" covering datasets, metrics, dimensions, relationships and contexts. New members at that point included AtScale, Coalesce, Collate, Credible, Databricks, JetBrains, Lightdash and Qlik, and governance was planned to move to a "neutral, foundation-led" model — [Snowflake blog (27 Jan 2026)](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/open-semantic-interchanges-specs-finalized/).
- **Version number conflict.** Secondary sources disagree on the version number. They variously describe the January 2026 release as v0.1, as v1.0, or with a current core of 0.1.1 — [Agile Brand Guide](https://agilebrandguide.com/wiki/data/open-semantic-interchange-osi/); [Datus.ai](https://datus.ai/blog/open-semantic-interchange-osi/); [UnwindData](https://unwinddata.com/osi-open-semantic-interchange-guide).
- **Move to Apache.** OSI reportedly entered the Apache Incubator on 10 July 2026 as "Apache Ossie (incubating)", with code at github.com/apache/ossie. This was found only in secondary sources and was not confirmed against an apache.org page — [snowflake.help (2026)](https://snowflake.help/apache-ossie-incubating-snowflakes-open-semantic-interchange-becomes-an-industry-standard/); [Datus.ai (2026)](https://datus.ai/blog/what-is-snowflake-osi/).
- **Participant count.** One Summit recap reports 54 participating vendors as of June 2026 — [Atlan recap (Jun 2026)](https://atlan.com/know/snowflake/summit-2026-announcements/).
- **AI context in the specification.** The specification carries AI context such as synonyms and usage instructions — [Datus.ai](https://datus.ai/blog/open-semantic-interchange-osi/).
- **Vendor support.** Databricks Metrics is described as "OSI ready", and dbt open-sourced MetricFlow explicitly in support of OSI — [Databricks (16 Jun 2026)](https://www.databricks.com/blog/whats-new-unity-catalog-data-ai-summit-2026); [dbt Labs (Oct 2025)](https://www.getdbt.com/blog/dbt-labs-affirms-commitment-to-open-semantic-interchange-by-open-sourcing-metricflow).
- **MCP adoption.** MCP is used by the dbt MCP server (GA), Atlan, Collibra, Alation, Looker, Google Knowledge Catalog, Glean, Databricks Unity AI Gateway's MCP registry and Tableau MCP — sources cited in the vendor sections above.
- **A2A.** Google uses A2A to publish Looker agents into Gemini Enterprise — [Google Cloud blog (2026)](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/business-intelligence/integrating-looker-and-gemini-enterprise).
- **OpenSharing.** Databricks positions OpenSharing, a Linux Foundation project, as a vendor-neutral protocol for sharing AI assets such as agent skills, models and unstructured data — [Databricks (16 Jun 2026)](https://www.databricks.com/blog/whats-new-unity-catalog-data-ai-summit-2026).
- **MCP alone is not enough.** A Gartner prediction, quoted by a consultancy and not verified against Gartner, says that 60% of agentic analytics projects relying solely on MCP will fail by 2028 without a consistent semantic layer — [Proventeq](https://www.proventeq.com/services/data-and-ai/salesforce-agentic-analytics).

### Inferences
- OSI addresses portability of metric semantics, but round trips are lossy. Snowflake-specific features go into vendor extensions, and non-equi joins can be dropped, so "portable" in practice means portable for the common core.
- MCP standardizes how agents reach context, but it says nothing about what the context means. That is why vendors pair MCP with OSI-style semantics.

- The status and roadmap of Apache Ossie were not confirmed on an Apache primary page.

## What do analysts and practitioners say about lock-in and consolidation?

### Takeaway
Analysts treat the context layer as a newly budgeted category for 2026–2027, and every catalog, warehouse and BI vendor is relabelling products to claim it. The main lock-in concern is that semantics embedded in one platform (semantic views, LookML, Tableau Semantics, Unity Catalog Metrics, Palantir Ontology) raise switching costs. OSI, open-sourced engines (MetricFlow, Spark Metrics) and neutral catalogs are the proposed mitigations. Much of this commentary comes from vendors with a stake in the outcome.

### Cited Findings
- **Gartner framework.** Gartner's framework defines a context layer through semantics, operational state and provenance. Atlan reports that Gartner's August 2026 Impact Radar names AI context platforms the most critical enabling technology for 2027. Both points come through Atlan's interpretation — [Atlan (2026)](https://atlan.com/know/core-components-context-layer/).
- **Budget line.** Gartner D&A 2026 is described as the point "where the context layer became a budget line item" — [Context and Chaos Substack (2026)](https://contextandchaos.substack.com/p/gartner-d-and-a-2026-where-the-context).
- **Sceptical view.** Joe Reis satirizes Gartner declaring "2026 the Year of Context", and argues that vendors are renaming active-metadata products as "context" products — [Joe Reis Substack (2026)](https://joereis.substack.com/p/gartner-declares-2026-the-year-of).
- **Adoption figures.** A conference recap cites figures that 44% of data and analytics leaders have implemented semantic layers and another 48% plan to by 2027 — [BigDATAwire, Semantic Layer Summit (2026)](https://www.hpcwire.com/bigdatawire/this-just-in/semantic-layer-summit-2026-spotlights-business-context-as-critical-infrastructure-for-enterprise-ai/).
- **Portability demand.** AtScale's summit messaging says that enterprises want semantic logic that moves across clouds, BI tools, models and apps "without locking business meaning into a single platform". AtScale is a semantic-layer vendor — [BigDATAwire (2026)](https://www.hpcwire.com/bigdatawire/this-just-in/semantic-layer-summit-2026-spotlights-business-context-as-critical-infrastructure-for-enterprise-ai/); [AtScale (2026)](https://www.atscale.com/blog/semantic-layers-critical-ai-infrastructure/).
- **Neutral versus platform-native catalogs.** Atlan argues for a neutral catalog over Google Knowledge Catalog for multi-cloud estates. This is a self-interested claim — [Atlan (2026)](https://atlan.com/know/ai-agent/gcp/google-knowledge-catalog-vs-neutral-catalog-bigquery/).
- **Competitor critiques.** Competitors critique LookML and Tableau Semantics as vendor-bound — [Strategy (2026)](https://software.strategy.com/blog/google-next-26-just-validated-agentic-bi-needs-a-semantic-layer); [SoftwareReviews (Oct 2026)](https://www.softwarereviews.com/vendor-technology-notes/dreamforce-2026-data-360-tableau-informatica).
- **Consolidation events.** Consolidation events include the dbt and Fivetran merger, announced in October 2025, and Neo4j's acquisition of GraphAware (deal value not found) — [Select Star (Oct 2025)](https://www.selectstar.com/resources/dbt-coalesce-2025); [Neo4j coverage (Oct 2026)](https://www.konsulteer.com/article/neo4j-launches-context-engine-to-give-enterprise-ai-agents-shared-business-knowledge). Flexera's Snowflake Summit 2026 recap headline refers to "rebrands, acquisition and 25+ announcements" — [Flexera (Jun 2026)](https://www.flexera.com/blog/perspectives/snowflake-summit-2026/).
- **Licensing shift.** dbt's move of MetricFlow from BSL to Apache 2.0 removed a licensing gate on production serving. Practitioners had cited that gate as a lock-in point — [Datus.ai (2026)](https://datus.ai/blog/dbt-semantic-layer-metricflow/).

### Inferences
- The lock-in risk has moved from data storage, where Iceberg and Delta are now open, to business semantics and agent runtime. Each platform's agent works best on its own semantic objects, even when those objects can be exported.
- In regulated financial firms, the practical trade-off is between deep platform-native governance plus agent integration (Snowflake, Databricks, Palantir) and portability through a neutral catalog plus an OSI-based approach (Atlan, Collibra, dbt), at the cost of an extra layer to operate.
